Talk to an AI voice agent in a Vonage Video call

Your browser and a Pipecat agent join the same Vonage Video session. The agent answers with OpenAI Realtime speech-to-speech.

You are talking to an AI, not a person. Its answers can be wrong. Your microphone audio travels through Vonage Video to OpenAI for the length of the call. This demo does not record anything.

Use headphones if you can, so the agent doesn't hear itself.

Ready when you are.

Built with the Vonage Video API, Pipecat, and OpenAI Realtime. The agent's audio reaches you through the Vonage Video session, never directly from OpenAI.
